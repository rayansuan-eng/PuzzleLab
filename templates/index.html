How to Play

1 Reach the target number. Build an equation whose result equals the target.
2 Use all four numbers exactly once. Every number tile must appear in your equation.
3 Combine the numbers. Use +, −, ×, ÷ and parentheses.
4 You have 6 valid attempts. Invalid or incomplete equations do not use an attempt.
